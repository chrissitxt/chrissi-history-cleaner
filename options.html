About

chrissi's History Cleaner never sends anything anywhere. There's no analytics, no tracking and no account. It only asks Chromium for four permissions, history, browsingData, alarms and storage, and none of them can read the content of the pages you visit. Your settings are saved with chrome.storage.local, so they stay on this device and are never synced. The source code is public on GitHub.
