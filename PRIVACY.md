# chrissi's History Cleaner privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

chrissi's History Cleaner never collects, sends or shares any data. Everything stays on your device.

## What the extension handles

- Your browsing history: the extension sees new visits through the `history` permission so it can delete them while history is paused. It only uses the visit time for this and does not store URLs or titles.
- Browsing data you choose to clear (history, cookies and other site data, cache, download history, autofill form data): it is deleted with the `browsingData` permission. Deleted data cannot be restored.
- Your settings: the selected options, the timer interval and the time of the last clear. They are saved with `chrome.storage.local` on your device and are never synced.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, no tracking, no accounts, no cookies
- No network requests and no remote code
- No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone
